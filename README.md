## 👩‍🔬 About me

I'm a **Bioinformatician and Computational Biologist** with expertise in **metagenomics, comparative genomics, transcriptomics, and microbial research**. I have research experience across **Japan, Italy, the USA, and Denmark**, bringing an international and interdisciplinary perspective to research and collaboration.

I focus on building **reproducible, scalable pipelines and tools** for robust biological analysis. I work with **large-scale sequencing datasets** on **high-performance computing** systems and develop **web-based platforms for data exploration**, applying data science methods at the intersection of bioinformatics and computational data analysis.

- 🧬 Currently a **Postdoctoral Researcher** at the [Center for Quantitative Genetics and Genomics (QGG)](https://qgg.au.dk/en/), Aarhus University
- 🌍 Research experience across **🇯🇵 Japan · 🇮🇹 Italy · 🇺🇸 USA · 🇩🇰 Denmark**
- 🔬 Passionate about computational approaches to complex biological questions, in **academia and industry**
- 💬 Ask me about **metagenomics, NGS/RNA-seq pipelines, and HPC workflows**

## 📌 Featured projects

🔬 **Rumen microbiome research — dairy cattle**
Genome-resolved **metagenomic and metatranscriptomic** analysis of the rumen microbiome, focusing on microbial composition, functional profiling, and **methane-related traits** in dairy cattle.

🧩 **MetaMAG Explorer**
A modular pipeline I developed for **MAG recovery, annotation, and reference-database integration** — an end-to-end workflow for genome-resolved metagenomics.
<!-- Add the repo link when public: [MetaMAG Explorer](https://github.com/msatti123/REPO) -->

🗂️ **Rumen-specific microbial reference database & gene catalogue**
Building a curated **reference database and gene catalogue** to improve taxonomic and functional profiling of rumen microbial communities.

🌱 **Methane-inhibition study**
Investigating how **methane inhibition** reshapes rumen microbial communities and fermentation pathways, using genome-resolved **metagenomics and metatranscriptomics**.

🖥️ **Interactive data-visualization platform**
A **web-based platform** for interactive exploration of biological data, built with **HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and SQL**.
