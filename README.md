## About

I'm a Bioinformatician and Computational Biologist with expertise in metagenomics, comparative genomics, transcriptomics, and microbial research. I have research experience across Japan, Italy, the USA, and Denmark, bringing an international and interdisciplinary perspective to research and collaboration.

I focus on building reproducible, scalable pipelines and tools for robust biological analysis. I work with large-scale sequencing datasets on high-performance computing systems and develop web-based platforms for data exploration, applying data science methods at the intersection of bioinformatics and computational data analysis.

- Currently a **Postdoctoral Researcher** at the [Center for Quantitative Genetics and Genomics (QGG)](https://qgg.au.dk/en/), Aarhus University
- Research experience across Japan, Italy, the USA, and Denmark
- Interested in computational approaches to complex biological questions, across academia and industry
- Ask me about metagenomics, NGS/RNA-seq pipelines, and HPC workflows

## Core strengths

- Metagenomic and metatranscriptomic data analysis
- End-to-end pipeline development for NGS and RNA-seq data
- MAG recovery, taxonomic classification, and functional annotation
- Microbial reference database and gene catalogue development
- Statistical analysis and data visualization in Python and R
- HPC workflow automation with Bash

## Featured projects

**Rumen microbiome research — dairy cattle.** Genome-resolved metagenomic and metatranscriptomic analysis of the rumen microbiome, focusing on microbial composition, functional profiling, and methane-related traits in dairy cattle.

**MetaMAG Explorer.** A modular pipeline I developed for MAG recovery, annotation, and reference-database integration — an end-to-end workflow for genome-resolved metagenomics.

**Rumen-specific microbial reference database & gene catalogue.** A curated reference database and gene catalogue to improve taxonomic and functional profiling of rumen microbial communities.

**Methane-inhibition study.** Investigating how methane inhibition reshapes rumen microbial communities and fermentation pathways, using genome-resolved metagenomics and metatranscriptomics.

**Interactive data-visualization platform.** A web-based platform for interactive exploration of biological data, built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and SQL.
